Trickle Down in beta

A daily trading game that is also a joke about trading. Every day you get $100 of fake money to put into real stocks and crypto. Returns are amplified ten times, because this is a game and not advice. Profits get taxed, the taxes hire lobbyists for charities, and the site's real ad money follows the lobbyists. It's in beta, which means some things are broken on purpose and some by accident.
