A bit about Erin

Erin is a software developer who started out as a scientist. At Grant Street Group she writes the code that moves government tax offices onto Grant Street's tax software, a job that's equal parts programming and careful detective work on messy data.

She earned a B.S. in microbiology at UT Austin, then spent three years in a San Francisco lab testing cannabis products for potency. She started as a technician preparing samples and finished as supervisor, leading a team of four analysts, running HPLC instruments, and developing and validating new test methods.

In 2022 she took time off to change careers, worked through Tech Elevator's full-stack Java bootcamp, and has been writing software professionally since 2023. The lab habits came with her.
