A bit about Erin

Erin is a software developer who started out as a scientist. At Grant Street Group she writes the code that moves government tax offices onto Grant Street's tax software, a job that is roughly equal parts programming and data forensics.

She earned a B.S. in microbiology at UT Austin, then spent three years in a San Francisco lab testing cannabis products for potency. She started as a technician preparing samples and finished as supervisor, leading a team of four analysts, running HPLC instruments, and developing and validating new test methods.

In 2022 she changed careers, completed Tech Elevator's full-stack Java bootcamp, and has been writing software professionally since 2023. She kept the lab habits.

Outside work, she's mom to one beautiful daughter. She swims for fun, never laps; reads hard-magic fantasy, biographies and biomedical stories; and plays video games.
