Remember watching your PC defragment? Now you run it.

How to play
  1. Each color is one file, broken into scattered pieces.
  2. Tap a glowing file. Its pieces stream into one solid block in the first gap big enough to hold it (the disk reads left to right, top to bottom).
  3. Files that don't fit anywhere yet are dimmed. Moving one file frees up space for others. Get every file in one piece.

Candy keeps coming and three kids are watching. Keep it fair.

How to play
  1. The outlined candy is next. Tap a jar to drop it in. You can see what's coming after it.
  2. When all three jars are exactly level at or above the dashed line, everyone cashes out and the jars empty.
  3. Higher cash-outs pay more. Overfill a jar and the run ends. The line creeps up as you go.

You're the driver, and every house on the block has an order.

How to play
  1. Drag from the van through neighboring houses that want the same color. Glowing houses are where a run can start.
  2. Let go and the van drives the run, dropping a package at each house. It parks where the run ends.
  3. Delivered houses place new orders. Runs of 5+ earn the van an extra run. Hit the delivery goal before you're out of runs.
