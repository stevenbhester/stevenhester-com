Stop 3 · the long haul

Then the maps get big.

Every few levels the road learns a new trick. By level 45 they're all on one map, and getting it in one sets the van on fire. That's the good outcome.

  • ONE-WAY STREETSTurn on from the side if you like. Leave the way the arrow points.
  • BRIDGESCross a junction twice: once over, once under.
  • COLOUR GATESThey only lift while the right package is on top.
  • PARCEL SHOPSPick up a parcel on the way, straight onto the top of your stack.
  • SORTING HUBSRoll over one and your top two packages swap.
  • BACK TO DEPOTDeliver everything, then make it home without crossing your own wreckage.
