Stop 2 · the rules

The whole game, in three rules

RULE 01THIS WAY UP

Top of the stack goes first.

Your load rides a conveyor belt in the order it has to go out. Every house wears a number, and 1 is the only door you can knock on right now.

RULE 02FRAGILE

The road breaks up behind you.

You drive so hard the asphalt gives up. No doubling back for the one you missed. Point the van at the wreckage and it stands on the brakes instead.

RULE 03DO NOT BEND

So plan the whole run.

Gates that only lift for the right package. Bridges. One-way streets. Parcel shops that mess with your stack. There's one perfect route, and you'll know when you've found it.

Stop 3 · the long haul

Then the maps get big.

Level 45 has eight packages, two bridges, two gates and a sorting hub that swaps your stack mid-run. Get it in one and the van catches fire. That's the good outcome.
